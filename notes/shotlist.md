# Recording session

## Setup
- Phone: landscape, 1080p 60 fps, propped at hip height, side-on to the fencing line, 4–5 m away on the 1x lens. Whole body in frame across the full distance you'll move.
- Watch on the weapon wrist, snug. Sensor Logger on the watch: Wrist Motion on, highest rate. Export as zipped CSV.
- Light from the front or side, not behind you.

## Every take
1. Start the watch recording, then the video.
2. Tap the watch wrist hard on a wall or table in frame.
3. Hold en garde, still, for 3 s.
4. Say each block's name out loud as you start it.
5. Hold en garde for 3 s at the end, then stop both.
Keep takes under 5 minutes; the watch caches about 5 minutes without the phone nearby.

## Takes
1. **Blocks**, 10 reps each: advance, retreat, advance-lunge, lunge to the wall target, extension, parry 4, parry 6, counter-6, septime, octave, beat-thrust, disengage-thrust, flèche (if there's room).
2. Repeat take 1 two more times. The repeats are what the model trains and tests on.
3. **Free fencing**: 2 takes of 1–2 minutes shadow-fencing as in a bout, mixing everything. These are held out for the demo.

Name each pair's folder after the take, e.g. `data/raw/home1/`, with `video.mov` and `watch.zip` inside.
